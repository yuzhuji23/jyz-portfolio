Avazu

Mobile ad click logs

DuckDB · Python

  • About 40.4 million impressions from the public Avazu click log, 21–30 October 2014. The question was how to drop low-value repeat impressions without giving up reach or clicks.
  • DuckDB SQL splits the click rate by channel, hour, and how many times a user has already been shown an ad. An A/A check, then an A/B design, for one rule: on app traffic over cellular, cap from the fourth impression. In the training window that kept 95.7% of impressions, raised the click rate 3.85%, and kept 99.4% of clicks. A later window held 96.8%, +2.42%, and 99.1% of clicks.
  • Logistic regression, FM, and DeepFM only show that clicks can be ranked. They are not the result. The log is public contest data, not an in-house ad system.
